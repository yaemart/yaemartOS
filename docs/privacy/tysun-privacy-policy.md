## 8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including:

- **Encryption in Transit**: TLS 1.2+ for all connections
- **Encryption at Rest**: AES-256 for database storage
- **Access Control**: Role-based access control (RBAC + ABAC) with principle of least privilege
- **Password Security**: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (minimum cost factor 12)
- **Audit Logging**: All data access and modifications are logged
- **Penetration Testing**: Annually [TO BE CONFIRMED — schedule with dev team]
- **Breach Notification**: We will notify affected users and relevant authorities within 72 hours of discovering a breach (GDPR Art. 33/34)
